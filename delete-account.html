删除喵喵刷题 / MeowQuiz 账号

开发者：Meow_Studio

你可以随时删除喵喵刷题账号。删除后不能恢复。

请注意：没用完的分钟和导入积分会作废，不能恢复、不退款，也不能转到别的账号。Google Play 购买的退款按 Google Play 的规则处理，我们不另外退款。

在 App 里删除

  1. 打开喵喵刷题，登录要删除的账号。
  2. 点“我的 → 我的账号 → 删除账号”。
  3. 输入密码，点“永久删除账号”。

通常几秒内删完，App 会显示“账号已删除”并退出登录。如果当时还有 AI 解题或导入整理在进行，App 会提示还没删完：等它结束后再点一次“永久删除账号”即可。

没装 App：发邮件申请

用你注册时的邮箱发邮件到 meowquizofficial@gmail.com，标题写“删除账号”。不要写密码或验证码。我们 7 天内回复，确认是本人后 30 天内删完，并发邮件告诉你。

会删除什么

账号（邮箱、密码、昵称、登录会话和登录记录）、余额，以及兑换、计时和 AI 用量记录。删除后所有设备都会退出登录。服务器上暂存的 AI 答案和整理结果也会一起删除。

会保留什么

  • 不含你邮箱和账号编号的账务记录和统计（例如哪天用了多少、扣了多少），长期保存，用于对账和防止滥用。
  • 服务器访问日志（可能含 IP 地址和账号编号）一般不超过 7 天自动删除；邮件服务商的发送记录按它的规则自动删除。
  • 你手机上的题库和做题记录不会自动删除，卸载 App 或清除应用数据即可删掉。

更多说明见隐私政策。有疑问请发邮件到 meowquizofficial@gmail.com。

Delete your MeowQuiz (喵喵刷题) account

Developer: Meow_Studio

You can delete your MeowQuiz account at any time. Deletion cannot be undone.

Please note: unused minutes and import credits are forfeited. They cannot be restored, refunded or moved to another account. Refunds for Google Play purchases follow Google Play’s rules; we do not issue separate refunds.

Delete in the app

  1. Open MeowQuiz and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap “Me → My account → Delete account” (我的 → 我的账号 → 删除账号).
  3. Enter your password and tap “Delete account permanently” (永久删除账号).

It usually finishes within a few seconds; the app shows “Account deleted” (账号已删除) and signs you out. If an AI answer or import sorting is still running, the app says deletion is not finished yet: wait for it to end, then tap “Delete account permanently” again.

No app? Ask by email

Email meowquizofficial@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with the subject “Delete account”. Do not include your password or any sign-in code. We reply within 7 days and, once we confirm it is you, finish the deletion within 30 days and let you know by email.

What we delete

Your account (email, password, nickname, login sessions and login records), your balance, and your redemption, timing and AI usage records. You are signed out on every device. Any AI answers or import results still held on our server are deleted too.

What we keep

  • Accounting records and statistics without your email or account ID (such as how much was used and charged on a given day), kept long term for bookkeeping and abuse prevention.
  • Server access logs (may include IP addresses and account IDs) are normally deleted automatically within 7 days; our email provider’s sending records are deleted under its rules.
  • The question bank and practice history on your phone are not deleted automatically; uninstall the app or clear its data to remove them.

See our Privacy Policy for more. Questions: meowquizofficial@gmail.com.
