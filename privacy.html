喵喵刷题 / MeowQuiz 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 20 日
运营方：Meow_Studio
联系邮箱：meowquizofficial@gmail.com

这份政策说明喵喵刷题 App（Android）会收集哪些信息、拿来做什么、交给谁、存多久，以及怎么删除。

  • 题库和做题记录只存在你手机里，不上传。
  • 只有你用 AI 解题或 AI 整理导入时，题目内容才会发到我们的服务器和 AI 服务商。
  • 无障碍权限只在你点“开始”后读取当前的题目。
  • 我们不卖你的数据，不放广告。
  • 你随时可以删除账号。

1. 我们收集什么、用来干什么

账号
邮箱、密码、昵称（可不填）和登录记录，用来登录和保存你的余额。密码只以加密散列保存。删除账号时要再输一次密码，它只用来核对是你本人，不保存、不记录。
屏幕上的题目（无障碍权限）
打开权限后 App 不会自动读。你打开悬浮窗、点“开始”后，App 才读取当前屏幕上的题目文字；不读密码框里的文字，也不会替你点击或输入。题目有图或文字读不全时，App 会截取整个屏幕（屏幕上的其他内容也会在里面），在手机上识别文字；截图只在内存里短暂保留，不保存。你随时可以点“暂停”，或在系统设置里关掉权限。
问 AI 的内容
解题方式选“题库 + AI”时，题库里查不到答案，或你点“问 AI”时，App 会把题目文字、选项和你题库里最多 3 道相似题发给 AI；打开“附图问 AI”（默认关闭）时附上截图。选“仅题库”就不发。服务器不保存这些内容，AI 的答案暂存 15 分钟后清空。
AI 整理导入
原文件不上传。你确认后，App 把在手机上拆出来的文字和图片分段发给 AI 整理。整理结果（含题目原文）在服务器上暂存 15 分钟后清空，整理好的题库只存在你手机上。
余额和用量
分钟余额、导入积分、兑换和计时记录，以及每次 AI 请求的用量和扣费，用来计费。这些记录不含题目内容。
Google Play 购买（接入后）
只接收 Google 提供的订单信息，用来到账、退款和对账。我们拿不到你的银行卡等付款信息。
你发来的邮件
用来回复你、处理你的请求。

App 用到的权限：无障碍（读题）、显示在其他应用上层（只用于开权限时的引导提示）、通知（导入进度和悬浮窗运行提示）、网络。

2. 交给谁处理

我们不卖你的信息。下面这些服务商替我们处理一部分信息：

  • Supabase：存账号、余额和用量记录，服务器在新加坡。隐私政策
  • OpenAI（默认）和 Anthropic（备用，OpenAI 连不上时使用）：AI 解题和整理导入时，收到题目内容（你允许时含截图），收不到你的邮箱或账号编号。OpenAI 隐私政策 · Anthropic 隐私政策
  • Resend：发送登录验证码等邮件，会收到你的邮箱，发送记录按它的规则自动删除。隐私政策
  • Google：App 在手机上用 Google ML Kit 识别截图文字，截图不上传；ML Kit 会向 Google 发送不含个人身份的诊断统计（说明）。通过 Google Play 购买时由 Google 处理付款。隐私政策

这些服务商可能在新加坡以外（例如美国）处理信息。法律要求时，我们会依法提供信息。

3. 存多久

  • 账号和登录记录：账号存在期间保存，删除账号时删除。
  • 发给 AI 的题目和截图：服务器不保存。AI 答案和整理结果：暂存 15 分钟。
  • 每次请求的用量明细：通常 90 天后合并成按天汇总；还没和 AI 服务商对完账的，保留到对账完成。
  • 余额、兑换、计时记录和用量汇总：账号存在期间保存。
  • 删除账号后：只留下不含邮箱和账号编号的账务记录和统计，长期保存，用于对账和防止滥用。
  • 服务器访问日志（可能含 IP 地址和账号编号）：一般不超过 7 天自动删除，删除账号时不会提前删除。
  • Google Play 订单记录（接入后）：保存到对账、退款和处理纠纷不再需要为止。
  • 服务商那边的记录：按各自的规则删除。你的邮件：处理完后可要求我们删除。

4. 安全

App 和服务器之间都用加密连接（HTTPS）。登录后你只能看到自己的数据。AI 服务的密钥只放在服务器上。

5. 你可以怎么做

  • 选“仅题库”，题目就不发给 AI；关掉“附图问 AI”，截图就不发；导入时不选 AI 整理，文件内容就不发；关掉无障碍权限，App 就读不到屏幕。
  • 删除账号：App 里“我的 → 我的账号 → 删除账号”，或看删除账号说明。
  • 想查看、更正或删除你的信息，或有疑问，发邮件到 meowquizofficial@gmail.com，我们 7 天内回复。

6. 政策更新

政策有变化时，我们会更新这个页面和生效日期；改动较大时，会提前在这个页面说明。

MeowQuiz Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026
Operator: Meow_Studio
Contact: meowquizofficial@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the MeowQuiz app (喵喵刷题, Android) collects, why, who processes it, how long we keep it, and how to delete it.

  • Your question bank and practice history stay on your phone.
  • Question content reaches our server and AI providers only when you ask AI or sort an import with AI.
  • The accessibility permission reads the current question only after you tap “Start”.
  • We do not sell your data and show no ads.
  • You can delete your account at any time.

1. What we collect and why

Account
Email, password, an optional nickname and login records, so you can sign in and keep your balance. Your password is stored only as an encrypted hash. When you delete your account you enter it again; it is used only to confirm it is you, and is not stored or logged.
Questions on screen (accessibility permission)
Turning the permission on does not start reading. Only after you open the floating panel and tap “Start” (开始) does the app read the question text on the current screen. It never reads the text in password fields and never taps or types for you. If a question has a picture or the text is incomplete, the app takes a screenshot of the whole screen (anything else on screen is included) and recognises the text on your phone. The screenshot is held briefly in memory and not saved. You can tap “Pause” (暂停) at any time or turn the permission off in system settings.
What is sent to AI
In “Bank + AI” (题库 + AI) mode, when your bank has no answer or you tap “Ask AI”, the app sends the question text, options and up to 3 similar questions from your bank to AI, plus the screenshot if “Attach image when asking AI” (附图问 AI, off by default) is on. In “Bank only” (仅题库) mode nothing is sent. Our server does not store this content, and holds the AI answer for 15 minutes before clearing it.
AI import sorting
The original file is never uploaded. After you confirm, the app sends the text and images it extracted on your phone to AI in pieces. The result (which includes the question text) is held on our server for 15 minutes and then cleared; the finished bank stays only on your phone.
Balance and usage
Minute balance, import credits, redemption and timing records, and the usage and charge of each AI request, for billing. These contain no question content.
Google Play purchases (once available)
We receive only the order information Google provides, to credit your account, handle refunds and keep accounts. We never see your card or other payment details.
Emails you send us
Used to reply and handle your request.

Permissions: accessibility (reading questions), display over other apps (only for the setup guide tip), notifications (import progress, floating panel running), network.

2. Who processes it

We do not sell your information. These providers process some of it for us:

  • Supabase: stores your account, balance and usage records, on servers in Singapore. Privacy policy
  • OpenAI (default) and Anthropic (backup, used when OpenAI cannot be reached): receive question content (with screenshots if you allow) for AI answers and import sorting, but not your email or account ID. OpenAI privacy policy · Anthropic privacy policy
  • Resend: sends sign-in code and other account emails, so it receives your email address; its sending records are deleted automatically under its rules. Privacy policy
  • Google: the app uses Google ML Kit to recognise text in screenshots on your phone, and screenshots are not uploaded; ML Kit sends Google diagnostic statistics that do not identify you (details). Google handles payment for Google Play purchases. Privacy policy

These providers may process information outside Singapore, for example in the United States. We disclose information where the law requires it.

3. How long we keep it

  • Account and login records: while your account exists; deleted when you delete it.
  • Questions and screenshots sent to AI: not stored on our server. AI answers and import results: held for 15 minutes.
  • Per-request usage details: normally merged into daily summaries after 90 days; requests not yet reconciled with the AI provider’s bill are kept until that is done.
  • Balance, redemption and timing records, and usage summaries: while your account exists.
  • After account deletion: only accounting records and statistics without your email or account ID, kept long term for bookkeeping and abuse prevention.
  • Server access logs (may include IP addresses and account IDs): normally deleted automatically within 7 days; deleting your account does not remove them sooner.
  • Google Play order records (once available): until no longer needed for bookkeeping, refunds and disputes.
  • Records at our providers: deleted under their own rules. Your emails: you can ask us to delete them once handled.

4. Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). When signed in you can see only your own data. AI service keys stay on the server.

5. Your choices

  • Use “Bank only” so questions are not sent to AI; turn off “Attach image when asking AI” so screenshots are not sent; skip AI sorting so file content is not sent; turn off the accessibility permission so the app cannot read the screen.
  • Delete your account in the app under “Me → My account → Delete account” (我的 → 我的账号 → 删除账号), or see Delete your account.
  • To see, correct or delete your information, or ask a question, email meowquizofficial@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days.

6. Changes

When this policy changes we update this page and the effective date. For significant changes we will say so on this page in advance.
